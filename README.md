# Diver Card Designer

Build your Helldivers 2 service card: name, title, level, loadout, avatar, diver and clan logo, plus your medals and ribbons, then save it as an image for Discord.

**Open it:** https://themoosen-lab.github.io/diver-card-designer/

Works on computers, phones and tablets. Your card is remembered in your browser between visits.
